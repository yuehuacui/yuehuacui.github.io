Research

Statistical methodology for genetics, genomics, and the biological sciences.

Statistical genetics

Genetic association studies; expression and quantitative trait locus mapping; gene–gene and gene–environment interactions; Mendelian randomization; and high-dimensional mediation analysis.

Statistical genomics

Methods for single-cell RNA sequencing and spatial transcriptomics, including spatially variable gene detection, spatial domain detection, cell type deconvolution, and gene network inference.

Statistical methods

Longitudinal data analysis, functional data analysis, and mixture models, with applications in the biological sciences.
