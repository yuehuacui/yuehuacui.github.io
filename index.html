About me

I am a Professor in the Department of Statistics and Probability at Michigan State University.

My research interests include statistical genetics, statistical genomics, single-cell RNA sequencing, and spatial transcriptomics. I also work on longitudinal and functional data analysis, mixture models, and statistical applications in the biological sciences.

I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of Florida in 2005 and joined Michigan State University the same year. I was elected a Fellow of the American Statistical Association in 2022.

Research interests

  • Statistical genetics: genetic association studies, (e)QTL mapping, gene–gene and gene–environment interactions, Mendelian randomization, and high-dimensional mediation analysis.
  • Statistical genomics: single-cell RNA-seq, spatial transcriptomics, and gene network inference.
  • Statistical methodology: longitudinal data analysis, functional data analysis, and mixture models.

Recent research

  • SpatialESD (2026): spatial ensemble domain detection in spatial transcriptomics. Advanced Science.
  • STANCE (2025): a unified statistical model for detecting cell-type-specific spatially variable genes. Nature Communications.
  • SPACE (2025): spatially variable gene clustering with adjustment for cell type effects. Nucleic Acids Research.
  • gwSPADE (2025): gene-frequency weighted reference-free deconvolution in spatial transcriptomics. Nucleic Acids Research.
